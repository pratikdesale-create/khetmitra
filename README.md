# 🌾 KhetMitra — From Seed to Sale

### AI-Powered Farmer Decision Support Platform

> **KhetMitra connects the farmer's decisions — not just the farmer's crop image.**

KhetMitra is a unified **AgriTech platform** designed to support farmers throughout the farming lifecycle — from **crop planning and health monitoring to market intelligence, profit estimation, buyer discovery, procurement tracking, and government scheme access**.

Unlike platforms that focus primarily on crop disease detection, KhetMitra aims to bring multiple farmer decisions together into **one connected digital ecosystem**.

---

## 🚜 Problem Statement

Farmers often need information from multiple disconnected sources while making decisions about their farms.

Common challenges include:

- 🌱 Difficulty in personalized crop planning
- 🩺 Delayed identification of crop-health problems
- 📍 Limited awareness of hyperlocal crop-health risks
- 💰 Fragmented mandi and market information
- 🤝 Limited visibility of potential buyers
- 📉 Multiple intermediaries can reduce farmer earnings and increase consumer prices
- 🚛 Long waiting times at procurement centers
- 📅 Lack of information about procurement schedules
- ❓ Uncertainty about procurement status
- 🏛️ Difficulty discovering relevant government schemes
- 📱 Limited accessibility in low-connectivity areas
- 🗣️ Difficulty using technology because of language and digital-literacy barriers

### The Core Problem

> Farmers don't just need information about their crops — they need **connected information to make better decisions throughout the farming lifecycle.**
